Privacy

Last updated November 15, 2026.

Quirk Atlas is a static map site from Jupiter Rockets. This page explains what this website does and does not collect.

No tracking

This site does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. It does not run a login or collect accounts. We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect a personal profile about you.

What happens in your browser

  • Map tiles are loaded from OpenFreeMap. MapLibre GL JS is loaded from a public CDN (unpkg).
  • Layer data for this site is served from this same website (this domain or the GitHub Pages host that serves it). Nothing is fetched from jupiterrockets.com to draw the map.
  • The Places search, used only when you submit it, sends the search text to OpenStreetMap Nominatim, limited to West Virginia, at most one request per second.
  • Near Me and the map’s locate button ask for your device location only when you tap them. The location stays in this browser so the map can show nearby places. We do not store it, and we do not send it to Jupiter Rockets or to this website’s host.
  • Road-trip routing is optional. When you tap “Find quirks along the way,” the start and end coordinates (which may be your location, a searched town, or a map tap) are sent to the FOSSGIS OSRM public routing server to calculate a driving route. FOSSGIS may log those requests; see their privacy policy. Nothing about the route is sent to us. If that server is unavailable, the page falls back to a straight-line corridor on your device and does not send the coordinates.
  • Share links are ordinary page addresses with a # hash, including #r= road-trip links. Copying or sharing one does not notify us.
  • A few layer preferences, such as unlocking the Pepperoni Roll Trail, may be saved in your browser’s local storage on this device only.

Data credits

The map and its layers use open or public sources. Please credit them if you reuse the data:

  • OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). The basemap, the West Virginia outline, Flock / ALPR camera locations, some place data, and driving routes are derived from OSM. Changed OSM-derived databases must also be shared under ODbL. Please fix the map if a road or place looks wrong.
  • Driving routes: Open Source Routing Machine (OSRM) via the FOSSGIS public demo (reasonable, non-commercial, non-heavy use; at most one request per second; no uptime guarantee). ⚠ This is a public demo, not a production SLA. If traffic grows or the app is sold, we should move to a self-hosted or paid router.
  • Map rendering: MapLibre GL JS. Tiles: OpenFreeMap / OpenMapTiles.
  • Mine disasters: WV Office of Miners’ Health, Safety & Training (OMHST), plus NIOSH and MSHA.
  • Mine and oil & gas context: West Virginia Geological and Economic Survey (WVGES).
  • Abandoned mine sites: WVDEP Abandoned Mine Lands.
  • Mineral sites: USGS Mineral Resources Data System (MRDS) (U.S. government work).
  • Geology (including planned bedrock context): Macrostrat (CC BY 4.0).
  • State parks, state forests, and wildlife management areas: West Virginia Division of Natural Resources public-land boundaries via the WV GIS Technical Center. ⚠ The public service does not state a reuse license; credit WVDNR and WVGISTC. Summersville Lake State Park’s point is from Wikidata (CC0).
  • Scenic byways and backways: West Virginia Department of Transportation Linear Referencing System (current scenic-byway events). ⚠ The service does not state a reuse license; credit WVDOT. National designations and some route descriptions: FHWA America’s Byways (U.S. government work).
  • Card descriptions and some coordinates also come from Wikidata (CC0) and Wikipedia introductions (CC BY-SA). Photos appear only when a Wikimedia Commons file is public domain, CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA; each card credits the author and license.

This map is not for engineering, legal, or safety use. Locations can be approximate.
