    About 10,000 abandoned mine land problem sites (open portals, shafts, dangerous impoundments and more) from the WV Department of Environmental Protection. Loads when turned on; visible when zoomed in.

    ⚠ Look, don't enter. Old mines can hold bad air, water and unstable ground. Never go into a mine opening, and don't trespass. Many sites are on private land.

    Well-known West Virginia whitewater runs, with public put-ins and take-outs and licensed commercial rafting outfitters. Run lines follow the USGS river map between official access points. Tap a run, access, or outfitter for class, season and sources. Default on.

    Public fishing access sites and boat ramps, trout-stocked streams, catch-and-release and fly-fishing-only waters, and public fishing lakes, from WVDNR GIS. Stocking frequency follows WVDNR codes; check the current stocking report and fishing regulations before you go. Gold shaded zones are official catch-and-release or fly-only drainages, including tributaries; zoom in to see the streams, and tap a zone for the official wording. Lakes and special-regulation streams show at the statewide view; access sites and other stocked streams fade in as you zoom. Derived and simplified — not an official WVDNR map. West Virginia Division of Natural Resources is the authoritative source for official hunting, fishing, and regulatory information. In case of discrepancies, the West Virginia Hunting and Fishing Regulations take precedence.

    Public taprooms and tasting rooms that are still operating, drawn from official WV Tourism, ABCA and Agriculture lists. Some vineyards are by appointment — the card says so. This is not every licensed producer, and it is not an official ABCA or Tourism map. Check hours before you go; tastings are 21+. Photos appear only when an openly licensed picture exists, with credit on the card.

              About this beta

              Quirk Atlas is a work in progress from Jupiter Rockets. We’re adding layers bit by bit, starting with West Virginia.

              The Breweries & Wineries layer pins public taprooms and tasting rooms from official state lists. It is not every licensed producer, and hours can change — check before you go.

              Locations can be approximate — each place card shows how precise the pin is, and names its sources. This map is not for engineering, legal or safety use.

              Sources include the West Virginia Geological and Economic Survey (WVGES), used for some mine-disaster locations and mining context. WVGES records draw on data from the federal Office of Surface Mining and the WV Office of Miners’ Health, Safety and Training. Rivers and streams come from the USGS National Hydrography Dataset (public domain). Whitewater run facts come from the National Park Service, WV Tourism, the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers release schedule, and the WVDNR Whitewater Commission; put-ins and take-outs use official WVDNR access sites, NPS-named accesses, and OpenStreetMap locations. Fishing sites are a simplified derivative of WVDNR Wildlife Resources Section GIS data (not an official WVDNR map; current fishing regulations take precedence). Other sources are named in the menu and on each place card.
